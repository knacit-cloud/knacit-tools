# Human Territory — an evidence-based classification / 人間の領域 — 証拠に基づく分類

What can only humans do and feel? A fine-grained classification built from peer-reviewed evidence — including the evidence *against* human uniqueness. Compiled from the 5-theme research log in [research/](research/); every claim traces to the [verified-citation ledger](research/README.md).

**Status: working draft.** Pillar sources are AI-checked against primaries (⚠️); final human verification pass ongoing. Numbers may be quoted; treat verdicts as strong hypotheses, not dogma.

## The method behind this document

We did not start from "humans are special" and collect supporting quotes. For each theme we actively searched for **counterevidence** — studies where AI matches or beats humans — and let the classification follow the evidence. Four of our own initial claims did not survive:

1. "Empathy is human-only" — false as stated. AI-written empathy is often rated *higher* (Ayers 2023; Ovsyannikova 2025; Howcroft 2025 meta-analysis).
2. "People only open up to humans" — false as stated. Stigmatizing facts flow *more* easily to machines (Lucas 2014).
3. "Humans read nonverbal cues" — misleading. Human deception detection averages 54% — barely above chance (Bond & DePaulo 2006).
4. "Humans elicit *deeper* disclosure than AI" — not supported. Peer-reviewed studies find AI interviewers match or beat humans on informativeness and specificity (Xiao 2020; Barari 2025; Wieland 2026). The human edge is *trust* and catching idiosyncratic specifics — not depth.

What survived is narrower, and stronger.

## The pattern

Across all five themes, the same shape emerged: **AI matches or beats humans wherever the task is expressible as text and judged on output. Human territory begins where value depends not on the output, but on the mode of being of the one who produces it** — being physically there, being in relation, having something at stake.

## The classification

### Humans only — in principle (model improvements don't change these)

| # | Capability | Why it stays human | Evidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| H1 | Being answerable for a judgment | Responsibility is a social status, not a skill; delegated to a machine it doesn't transfer — it vanishes ("irreducibly human") | [Radanliev 2026](https://doi.org/10.3389/frai.2026.1800302) ⚠️ |
| H2 | Staking oneself — a judgment backed by someone who can feel regret, loss, disappointment | AI cannot suffer its own failure, and the other party knows it; trust is built on cost | ⚠️ **partially sourced (upgraded 2026-07-25).** Closest direct evidence: [Fahnenstich, Rieger & Roesler, *CHB* 153:108107, 2024](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.chb.2023.108107) — people rate a **human** support agent as **more responsible for possible negative outcomes** than an AI agent (same task). That supports "the other party knows who carries the cost," **but not** the affective claim that AI cannot feel regret. Also: costly-signaling ([Yu, Berg & Zlatev 2021](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S0749597821000194)) — human–human only. Note: Radanliev 2026 does **not** support this row (verified) |
| H3 | Physically grounding cognition in a situation (five senses, plus awareness of one's own internal state) | Cognition is structured by having a body; not fixable by adding sensors | [Kadambi et al., Neuron 2026](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.neuron.2026.03.004) ✅; [Ziemke 2016](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S030326471630168X) ✅ — Kadambi verified 2026-07-27 against the published Neuron version (genre: **Perspective**, open access CC BY): the paper splits embodiment into *internal* (interoception, homeostatic regulation — its own examples are hunger, thermoregulation, blood glucose, water balance) and *external* (physical interaction), and argues internal embodiment is the neglected one. **"Fatigue" and "felt uncertainty" are our extension, not the paper's examples. Note also that Kadambi does *not* argue AI is barred from this in principle** — it operationalizes internal embodiment functionally and says it "remains agnostic about whether artificial systems similarly require such internal, subjective experiences," proposing functional analogs instead. The "not fixable by adding sensors" framing rests on Ziemke, not Kadambi. Ziemke was human-verified 2026-07-27: a peer-reviewed review article (not opinion) synthesizing multiple frameworks — autopoiesis, Christensen & Hooker's autonomy theory, Bickhard's interactivism, Damasio/Panksepp's somatic theories — that converge on homeostatic/allostatic self-regulation grounding embodied cognition; it's open access under CC BY-NC-ND, not paywalled as previously noted |
| H4 | Somatic tacit knowledge (skills carried in the body) | Cannot be rendered propositional → can never appear in training data | [Collins taxonomy (Soler & Zwart 2013)](https://journals.openedition.org/philosophiascientiae/892) ✅ (human-verified 2026-09-04) |
| H5 | Collective tacit knowledge (know-how living in shared practices) | Extractable from no individual; transmitted only by participation | [Collins taxonomy](https://journals.openedition.org/philosophiascientiae/892) ✅ (human-verified 2026-09-04); [med-ed editorial (Cureus 2026)](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12927663/) ⚠️ |

### Humans in practice — relational advantages (robust today, theory-backed)

| # | Capability | Precise statement | Evidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| H6 | Being felt as genuinely caring — a *belief*, not a quality gap | Identical words are worth more from a human; the mere suspicion "an AI helped" lowers felt empathy, even under a human label — belief, not authorship, drives it. Effect is confined to affective/motivational empathy (cognitive empathy shows no gap). **Choice-evaluation paradox:** people consistently prefer receiving empathy from a human (57–62%) even though they rate AI's response as higher-quality and *more* effortful — cost-signaling alone doesn't explain the preference | [Rubin 2025](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41562-025-02247-w) ⚠️; [Wenger et al. 2026](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12872445/) ⚠️ |
| H7 | Eliciting disclosure — *trust*, not raw depth | **Reframed (theme-4 rebuild)** — "humans elicit *deeper* accounts" is not supported; peer-reviewed evidence leans AI-favorable on informativeness (Xiao 2020; Barari 2025; Wieland 2026). What survives: people trust human interactants more (Croes 2024), and current LLMs miss idiosyncratic specifics / "richness" (Cuevas 2025) | [Cuevas 2025](https://arxiv.org/abs/2309.10187); [Xiao 2020](https://arxiv.org/abs/1905.10700); [Croes 2024](https://academic.oup.com/iwc/article/36/5/279/7692197) ⚠️ |
| H8 | Real-time relational adjustment | Adjusting distance, timing, and follow-up questions to the person in front of you — a *resonance* function, **not** lie detection (humans: 54%) | **negative half:** [Bond & DePaulo via Levine 2021](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8333997/) ✅ human-verified 2026-07-27 (54% ≈ chance → this is *not* detection; Levine adds that outside the lab, with research confederates, "accuracy is near zero, not 54%" — the lab figure is a ceiling, not a floor). **positive half, NEW 2026-07-25:** [Esposito et al., *Research in Psychotherapy* 27(2), 2024](https://doi.org/10.4081/ripppo.2024.751) ⚠️ — "therapist responsiveness" is an established, outcome-relevant construct (overlapping attunement/flexibility/empathy), though the review's own finding is that **operationalization is still heterogeneous and contested**. Open access |
| H10 | Feeling one's own unease as a signal | "Something is off here" — an internal bodily signal, to be treated as a hypothesis to test, never a verdict | **NEW 2026-07-25** — interoception (perceiving internal bodily states) is a real, studied capacity linked to decision-making ([Desmedt et al., *Neurosci. Biobehav. Rev.* 153:105388, 2023](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.neubiorev.2023.105388)) ⚠️. **Crucially, that review's own conclusion is that measuring interoceptive accuracy validly may be "elusive"** — which supports our stance exactly: a felt signal is a *hypothesis to test*, never a verdict |

### Standing warnings (about humans, not AI)

| # | Warning | Evidence |
|---|---|---|

## One-line synthesis

> **AI wins on output; humans are irreplaceable in presence, relation, and stake — and the greatest risk is not AI's limits but humans surrendering verification, thinking, and responsibility.**

This is why [Cross-Analysis](README.md) assigns the A-axis (data) to AI-assisted work under human verification, and the B-axis (field reality) to humans end to end — not as sentiment, but as the configuration the evidence supports.

# 人間の領域（日本語版）

人間にしかできない・感じられないことは何か。査読研究——**人間の独自性に不利な研究を含む**——から組み上げた細分類。5テーマの調査記録は [research/](research/)、全主張の出典は[検証台帳](research/README.md)にある。

**状態：作業草稿。** 柱となる出典は一次情報とのAI照合済み（⚠️）、人間による最終確認を進行中。

## この文書の作り方

「人間は特別だ」から出発して都合のいい引用を集めたのではない。各テーマで**反証**——AIが人間に並ぶ・勝る研究——を積極的に探し、分類は証拠に従わせた。私たち自身の最初の主張のうち4つは生き残らなかった：

1. 「共感は人間だけ」——そのままでは偽。AIの書く共感はしばしば人間より高評価（Ayers 2023；Ovsyannikova 2025；Howcroft 2025メタ分析）。
2. 「本音は人間にしか出さない」——そのままでは偽。恥ずかしい事実はむしろ機械に流れる（Lucas 2014）。
3. 「人間は非言語を読める」——誤解を招く。人間の欺瞞検出は平均54%＝ほぼ偶然（Bond & DePaulo 2006）。
4. 「人間の方が"深く"引き出す」——非支持。査読研究はAIインタビュアーが情報量・具体性で人間に並ぶ・勝ると示す（Xiao 2020；Barari 2025；Wieland 2026）。人間の優位は"深さ"でなく**信頼**と**固有の具体の察知**。

生き残ったものは、より狭く、より強い。

## 貫くパターン

5テーマすべてで同じ形が現れた：**タスクがテキストで表現でき、出力で評価されるかぎり、AIは人間に並ぶか勝る。人間の領域は、価値が出力ではなく「産み出す者の在り方」に依存するところから始まる**——身体でそこに居ること、関係の中に居ること、何かを賭けていること。

| H5 | 集合的暗黙知（実践に宿るノウハウ） | どの個人からも抽出できず、参加によってのみ伝わる | [Collins分類](https://journals.openedition.org/philosophiascientiae/892) ✅（2026-09-04人間確認済み）；[医学教育論説（Cureus 2026）](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12927663/) ⚠️ |

## 一行の統合

> **出力ではAIが勝つ。人間が代替不能なのは「そこに居ること・関係を結ぶこと・自分を賭けること」。そして最大のリスクはAIの限界ではなく、人間が検証と思考と責任を明け渡すことにある。**

だから[クロス分析](README.md)は、A軸（データ）を「人間の検証つきのAI補助」に、B軸（現場の現実）を「端から端まで人間」に割り当てる。感傷ではなく、証拠が支持する構成として。
